Your Path to Earning a Private Pilot's License

When I started flight training, most of what I found online was scattered, outdated, or contradicted something else I had just read. This site is the guide I wish I had on day one. It lays out what it actually takes to go from never touching the controls to holding a Private Pilot License, including the steps, the time, and the mistakes worth avoiding.

It's written for anyone roughly 16 to early 30s who is curious about flying but isn't sure where to start. No prior aviation knowledge needed.

Licensing Pathway

The steps from zero to PPL: medical certificate, student certificate, solo, knowledge test, and checkride.
